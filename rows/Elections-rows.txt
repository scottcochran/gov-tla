# Constants. The defaults are the smallest that run a whole election: three seats in the college, every elector
# held to the pledge (so a ballot is the pledge and the other candidate), two candidates, nobody mortal, disabled
# or in office, no vote open to objection, three States and three Senators. 38,180 states. The persons c, e4 and
# e5 are in Persons for the rows that add a candidate or a seat; unused, they add no state.
#
# Each mutation runs against every property at the constants where its check binds, so that a HOLDS in its block
# says the mutation is live and leaves that property alone:
#   M_OFFICER_ELECTOR   e3 holds an office (unmutated, that seat is never filled)
#   M_HABITATION        e1 shares a State with both candidates (unmutated, e1 can cast no ballot)
#   M_PRESIDING         e1's vote is open to objection
#   M_HOUSE_QUORUM      five States, the fewest at which a majority of all the States can lack a quorum
#   M_HOUSE_PRESENT     four States, the fewest at which a majority of a quorum is not a majority of all
#   M_SENATE_QUORUM, M_SENATE_PRESENT   the same, with Senators
#   M_UNQUALIFIED, M_XX3_BECOMES, M_SPEAKER_FIRST   b is not eligible (so b elected is a President elect who fails to qualify)
#   M_XIV3              b is under the disability
#   M_XXII              constraint ElectionsBound (elected <= 3), the count of elections being otherwise unbounded
#   M_XXII_SERVICE      one seat and b mortal: b dies early in a term and a serves out more than two years of it
# The other mutations bind at the defaults. Safety and the deadlock audit also run unmutated at each of these
# sets of constants (the c- rows), so a COUNTEREXAMPLE in a mutation's block is the mutation's and not the
# constants'. Two further c- rows are larger: three candidates (1,301,661 states), and the constants of
# Elections.cfg (2,060,728 states).
#
# CountCanClose is the one property no single guard decides. M_PRE_XII and M_PLURALITY leave it alone with two
# candidates and break it with three (m-prexii-canclose-3, m-plurality-canclose-3; the second needs four seats).
# Unmutated it is checked with three candidates and three seats (p-none-canclose-3) and with two candidates and
# four seats (c-four2-safety); three candidates with four seats, unmutated, is too large for a row.
#
# The quorum rules bind only at some sizes. With three or four States (or six) a majority of all the States is
# already two-thirds of them, so M_HOUSE_QUORUM changes nothing there: m-housequorum-house-3 and -4 HOLD, with the
# state count of none. With three or five States a majority of any quorum is a majority of all the States, so
# M_HOUSE_PRESENT's flag is never raised there: m-housepresent-house-3 and -5 HOLD. The same for the Senate.
#
# What the rows cannot reach: the House's list of the three highest excluding a fourth person voted for (seven
# seats, four candidates). The Senate's list of two is exercised by w-offsenatelist (five seats, three candidates).
#
# DEADLOCK: each stage of an election can be closed, noon comes once the count is open, and Midterm and
# OpenElection follow, so no state is terminal; d-none-deadlock and the c-...-deadlock rows check it.
